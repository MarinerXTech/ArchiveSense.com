Catalog, research, and publish

Capture your collection with the ArchiveSense app

The Android app keeps the object, its images, facts, notes, and emerging interpretation together. From there, selected records can become a public catalog and provide the material for a curated exhibition.

    Catalog what you know

    Build structured records with photographs, dates, descriptions, and tags.

    Develop what it means

    Use focused AI conversations to question an object and shape a collector’s note.

  3. 03
    Publish with intention

    Select what belongs in public, then share a complete collection or connect objects through an exhibition.
