Cutting-speed source & method

Published speeds depend on tool geometry, work hardness, feed, depth of cut, coolant and rigidity. The application's starting presets are workshop judgments, not direct quotations from the source table. Finishing (×1.12) and parting (×0.65) are app rules of thumb.

Your Machinery's Handbook, 23rd ed.: Machining Operations → Cutting Speeds and Feeds → Speed and Feed Tables (find exact page in the book's index).

RPM = 12 × SFM ÷ (π × diameter in inches); the assistant chooses the highest available spindle speed at or below the target. When no available speed is slow enough, it identifies that limitation.
